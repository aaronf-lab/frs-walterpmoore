How we work together

Six steps, each one earning the next

Every step keeps the engineering opinion and the client relationship with Walter P Moore. We bring evidence and field capacity, and we give first.
A

Give first: the no-cost 25-building pilot

What Walter P Moore gets

One storm, 25 WPM-chosen buildings. By day 3: triage table (Strong / Moderate / Radar-only / Clear), a one-page record per building, a swath map, methods. By day 14: field documentation to WPM’s protocol, then a joint review. We also offer on-site walks of the top 5 or top 10 pilot buildings with your engineers. No cost; WPM holds every client.

What we need

A past storm date, 25 addresses, one named contact, the written field protocol, site access; a mutual NDA first if client data is involved.

First next step

Walter P Moore names the storm, the 25 buildings and a contact; we agree whether to walk the top 5 or top 10.

A packaged due-diligence storm and roof file for each site, built to drop into Gary’s pre-purchase audits and repair/capital estimates. The items we review are listed below.

Active projects, site contacts, safety and access rules, and WPM’s QA checklist.
